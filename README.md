## WHAT IS THIS

A cinematic, immersive audio diagnostic web application.

You drop a mix in. The system scans it. You get a full trauma report — low-end fractures, stereo field collapse risk, harmonic contamination, transient damage — all delivered through a dark, surgical, emergency-room interface built for producers who take their sound seriously.

No softness. No shortcuts. Only results.

## FEATURES

### 🩺 Patient Intake
Register before you inject your signal. Alias, DAW, genre, hours spent, emotional state. The system personalises your entire session around your intake data.

### 📡 Signal Injection & Waveform Scan
Upload any audio file. Watch the waveform render in real time as the scanner locks onto your signal. Emergency lights pulse. The lab comes alive.

### ☣️ Trauma Analysis Engine
Multi-step diagnostic scan across 7 signal pathways. Results delivered with animated typing, severity tags (CRITICAL / WARNING / STABLE), and live damage meters.

**Example findings:**
- `LOW-END FRACTURE DETECTED`
- `STEREO FIELD COLLAPSE RISK`
- `HARMONIC CONTAMINATION`
- `EMOTIONAL CLARITY LOSS`
- `BUS COMPRESSION OVERLOAD`

### 🔗 Emergency Recovery Chain
Randomised surgical plugin chain suggestions with full EQ prescriptions. Every session gives different results.

### ⚡ Stabilize Audio
One button. Screen flashes red. Siren pulse activates. Final trauma grade drops.

### 📻 Audio Hospital Radio
Procedurally generated dark ambient drone — built live with Tone.js. Layered sine pads, brown noise texture, reverb and delay. 5 tracks. Runs in the background while you work.

### 📁 Case File History
Every completed scan is archived locally. Last 5 sessions stored — producer alias, filename, grade, trauma count, genre, date. Slide-out panel on the left edge.

### 💬 WhatsApp Booking
When the scanner flags critical damage, a booking panel drops in. Select your service. One tap opens WhatsApp with a pre-written message — alias, genre, DAW, and selected service pre-filled. Direct line to Smokey Eye Beats.

### 🛒 Plan B — Sonic Arsenal
If the scan doesn't finish you, the VSTs will. Full Audio Hospital weapon catalogue linked directly from the interface.
